# Payment Methods

## Accepted payment types
The company accepts the following payment methods at checkout:
- Major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover)
- Digital wallets: Apple Pay and Google Pay
- PayPal

## Buy now, pay later
For orders over **$35**, customers can choose a buy-now-pay-later option at checkout, processed through a third-party BNPL provider. This splits the order total into installments; the company itself does not manage the installment schedule or collect installment payments directly.

## Gift cards and store credit
Gift cards and store credit can be applied at checkout and combined with one other payment method to cover any remaining balance. Gift cards do not expire and have no additional fees.

## When payment is charged
Payment is charged in full at the time the order is placed, not at the time of shipment. If an item in a multi-item order is later found to be out of stock, the corresponding amount is refunded to the original payment method.

## Currency and restrictions
All transactions are processed in the customer's local currency at checkout. The company does not accept cash on delivery, checks, or direct bank transfers.
