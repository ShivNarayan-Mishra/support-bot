# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Order processing time
Orders are processed for **1–2 business days** before they ship. Delivery estimates below begin counting from the ship date, not the order date.

## Shipping options and delivery times
| Method | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 5–7 business days | $4.99, free on orders over $50 |
| Expedited | 2–3 business days | $12.99 |
| Overnight | 1 business day | $24.99, order must be placed before 12:00 PM local time |

## Tracking
A tracking number is emailed to the customer as soon as the order ships. Tracking can also be viewed from the account order-history page.

## Shipping restrictions
- Overnight shipping is not available to PO boxes.
- International shipping is available to a limited set of countries; availability is shown at checkout based on delivery address.
- Delivery times may be delayed during holidays or severe weather; such delays are outside the company's control and are not eligible for shipping fee refunds.

## Lost or delayed packages
If a package shows as delivered but was not received, or has not moved for more than 3 business days past the estimated delivery window, the customer should contact support to open a carrier investigation.
