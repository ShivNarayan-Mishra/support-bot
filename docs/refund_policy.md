# Refund Policy

## Return window
Customers may return most items within **30 days of the delivery date** for a full refund. The return must be initiated through the account order-history page before the window closes.

## Item condition
Returned items must be unused, in their original packaging, with all tags and manufacturer seals intact. Items missing original packaging or showing signs of use may receive a partial refund at the company's discretion.

## Refund processing
Once a returned item is received and inspected, refunds are issued to the original payment method within **5–7 business days**. The company does not issue cash refunds for card or digital wallet purchases.

## Return shipping costs
- If the item arrived damaged, defective, or incorrect, the company covers return shipping and refunds the original shipping cost in full.
- If the return is for any other reason (changed mind, wrong size, etc.), return shipping is paid by the customer and is deducted from the refund unless the order qualified for free shipping at purchase.

## Non-returnable items
The following categories are final sale and not eligible for refund:
- Digital downloads and gift cards
- Perishable goods
- Personal care or hygiene items with a broken seal
- Items marked "final sale" at checkout
